## 🐾 About me

Technology professional with 10+ years of experience in digital products, connecting **Product Design, UX/UI, User Research, Data Analytics, Requirements Engineering and Software Quality Assurance**. I work across the product lifecycle — from discovery and specification to prototyping, validation, metrics analysis and test automation.

I also research Computer Engineering, with interests in **Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Time Series Analysis, IoT and Computational Intelligence**. My goal is to turn complex challenges into useful, user-centered and data-informed solutions.
